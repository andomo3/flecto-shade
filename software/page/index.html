What this model does — and what the CAD establishes

The STEP establishes 22 flap instances in two overlapping height layers and one base. A 25 mm circular rain aperture centred 25 mm along local −Y from each flap origin is an assumption, based on the approximate rim size and the team's description. The display uses the prototype's actual scale; water volumes are not farm-scale projections.

The controller treats these as independent apertures. It does not calculate obstruction by the other flap layer, base, or supports. It rasterizes circles by cell centres, clips them to the rectangular map, and admits rain over the union of open masks. Zero outside delivery means zero at this grid resolution; it does not establish physical zero spill.

Uniform vertical rain; instantaneous switching; binary transmission; 60 mm soil capacity. No wind, runoff redistribution, evaporation, crop response, actuators, or structural deformation. The deterministic greedy choice is not an optimization proof. Animation speed is illustrative. All inputs, states, and outputs in this flow are simulated.

One roof, different choices for the areas beneath it.
