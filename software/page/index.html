The fin is the Flectofin, by ITKE, University of Stuttgart, patented as EP2320015. This is a physically informed browser approximation of that mechanism, not a validated mechanical or structural result. Mounting is drawn as a conceptual clamp.

About this model, and how to move around it

A modelled commercial shade house. Columns carry the primary beams, the beams carry the secondary rails, and every Flectofin module clamps to the two rails that bound its square bay. The planting below is drawn deterministically as decoration and is not a simulated crop response.

Drag to orbit. Shift and drag to pan. Scroll to zoom. Arrow keys turn the model, 1 to 4 select a zone, 0 resets the view. The hour only moves while the simulated day runs.
