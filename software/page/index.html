Model assumptions and CAD provenance

The STEP establishes 22 flap instances in two overlapping height layers and one base. A 25 mm circular rain aperture centred 25 mm along local −Y from each flap origin is an assumption, based on the approximate rim size and the team's description. The display uses the prototype's actual scale; water volumes are not farm-scale projections.

The controller treats these as independent apertures. It does not calculate obstruction by the other flap layer, base, or supports. It rasterizes circles by cell centres, clips them to the rectangular map, and admits rain once per cell when any covering aperture is open.

Strict protection can leave selected cells dry when an aperture would also wet an unselected cell. No wind, runoff redistribution, evaporation, crop response, actuators, or structural deformation. The deterministic greedy choice is not an optimization proof. Animation speed is illustrative. All inputs, states, and outputs in this flow are simulated.

One roof, different choices for the areas beneath it.
