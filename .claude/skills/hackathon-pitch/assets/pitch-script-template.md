## 60 seconds, 150 to 180 words

"[User] currently deals with [problem]. Existing solutions are hard because [reason]. We built [name], which [core action]. In the demo, you will see [flow]. This matters because [impact]."

## 3 minutes, 450 to 540 words

| Time | Say | Show | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:25 | One sentence problem and who feels it | Problem slide, one stat or quote | Origin story, team intros |
| 0:25 to 1:00 | The solution in one line and the core loop | Live product, golden path only | Feature list, architecture |
| 1:00 to 1:50 | Live demo, narrated click by click | Real data, seeded account | Typing, sign up flows, logs |
| 1:50 to 2:25 | Why it matters and what was validated | Before and after, one metric | "AI powered" without proof |
| 2:25 to 3:00 | What is next and the ask | Roadmap, three items, live link and QR | Overpromising, ending on "questions?" |

## Recovery lines

- Demo crashed: "Technology keeps us humble. Here is a thirty second clip of it working." Play the backup. Apologise once.
- Wifi died: switch to hotspot, then screenshots and the architecture.
- Forgot the line: "Let me restate that." Go back to the last clear point.
- Cannot answer: "Great question. We did not get to that in the build, but here is how I would approach it." Fifteen seconds, then stop.
- Slower than expected: narrate what the user is seeing while it loads.

## Q&A bank

Answer formula: direct answer in 10 seconds, evidence in 20, scope honesty in 10. Never bluff a live capability, offer the recording.

1. Who is this for, specifically?
2. What problem does it solve that existing tools do not?
3. How did you validate anyone wants this?
4. What did you build versus what is mocked?
5. What breaks if 100 users try this now?
6. How does the AI part work: model, prompt, fallback?
7. What happens when the API is down or rate limited?
8. Where is data stored and who can see it?
9. Why this stack, what did it let you ship faster?
10. What would you cut with 4 fewer hours?
11. What would you build with 4 more weeks?
12. How would you get your first 100 users?
13. What did each teammate own?
14. What was the hardest bug and how did you fix it?
15. Can I try it myself right now?
