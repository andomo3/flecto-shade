# frame-rig - local memory

Owner: the structure and rig role, one of the two engineers.

- The rig is foam board: the front view frame of one miniature bus stop with a bench, the servo box, the mount points for the leaf assembly, and the LED sun mount above.
- It is built in hours 6 to 8, in parallel if anyone is free, which with two engineers is unlikely, so it may slip behind the three leaf assembly.
- The LED must have a clear line of sight to the incident photoresistor, and the bench light sensor sits on the bench looking up at the roof.
- The ideal sun is the LEDs on a servo mount so the sun moves across the stop.
  It is decided at hour 8, from the parts and the time available, and the pivot is a fixed LED mount.
  Build the mount so either works: a fixed bracket that a servo horn can replace.
- The backup fixed roof is one more hour of foam board beside the canopy with its own bench sensor.
  It is built only if the core loop is green at hour 12 and someone is free.
- `linkage.md` is the contract with `../leaves/`: the backbone mount points, the pull wire or rigid link, the servo horn, and the travel angle.
  The structure owner writes it and the mechanism owner signs it before either side prints.
- Wiring is routed and secured with zip ties or tape in hours 12 to 14, because the move from the bench to the rig is where things break.
- The rig has to survive being carried to the judging table, so the final hour includes securing it for transport.

Record here at the event: photographs of the LED mount and both sensor positions, so the geometry survives a rebuild.
