# leaves - local memory

Owner: the mechanism role, one of the two engineers.

- The leaf is a backbone with a thin lamina that buckles sideways when the backbone bends, after the Flectofin principle, with no hinge anywhere.
- The starting point is a 2 mm backbone and a 0.5 mm lamina with a slight built in resting curve, the imperfection seed that makes it buckle the intended way.
- Two or three thickness combinations are printed or cut and flexed by hand in hours 0 to 2, and the prints are queued first because printing is the biggest passive wait.
- The venue print queue closes Saturday night, so every printed part is submitted by Saturday afternoon.
- Three leaves share one backbone and one servo, through a pull wire or a rigid link.
- The working assumption is that the flared leaf shades the bench, to be confirmed by the engineers.

Pivots, with their hours:

- Hour 2, nothing buckles cleanly: adjust the thickness ratio and reprint the best one or two, do not test every variant.
- Hour 4, still nothing: a foam board or flat cut lamina on the best backbone.
- Hour 4, the servo cannot reproduce the hand buckle: change the link geometry, and at hour 5 the leaf is hand actuated for the demo.
- Hour 6, three leaves bind or fall out of sync: two leaves, then one.

Record here at the event: the variant table with the time of each test, the settings that worked, and the milestone export names.
The backbone mount points and the link to the servo are fixed by `../frame-rig/linkage.md`.
