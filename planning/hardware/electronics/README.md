# Electronics

Owner: the electronics role.

What goes here:

- `pinout.md`, a hotspot file: every pin, the photoresistor and its line of sight to the LED sun, the flex sensor, the two temperature sensors, the LED's PWM pin, the servo supply, and the common ground.
  The structure owner signs the sensor positions, because they sit inside the rig.
- The wiring diagram, a photo is fine.
- Power notes: the SG90 runs from the external 5 V supply, never the board's 5 V pin.
- The board to laptop path for the UNO Q: D0 and D1 through a level shifter to a USB serial adapter, or a second Uno as the bridge.
- The sensor range test and the servo power test results, with the date.
