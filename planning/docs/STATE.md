## Standing decisions

- 2026-09-19, about 16:45: The simulated smart louvre roof is chosen, and it is frozen at the 17:00 standup.
  After the freeze a change is a cut, never a pivot.
- 2026-09-19: The setting is a shade house in the Apopka area of Central Florida, with the 2023 sun from NASA POWER and the 2023 rain from the Orlando Executive Airport gauge.
- 2026-09-19: The roof follows written, deterministic rules per zone, with no language model and no learned model in the loop, and the fins never track the sun's position.
- 2026-09-19: Nothing is measured, except the rain gauge's own record, and every figure says simulated or modelled.
- 2026-09-19, evening: Nothing physical is built, so the project is the simulation, the pitch, and the story.
- 2026-09-19: The fin is ITKE's Flectofin, patented as EP2320015, and every page and every pitch credits it.
- 2026-09-19: `../../AGENTS.md` at the repo root is the only rules file, `../../CHECKLIST.md` is the live board, and `../RUN.md` holds the decisions.
