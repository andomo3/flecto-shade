# Meeting 2026-09-11

The team chose the adaptive bus stop canopy and switched from choosing an idea to planning a working prototype, starting from the 90 second demo and working backwards.

## Decisions

- The adaptive bus stop canopy is the project, because the team prefers the Sustainability story and the visible mechanism, and it is the finalist with the most headroom under the v2 scoring.
- The impact insert and the termite mound testbed stay shortlisted as backups, because each has a finished build plan and the canopy still carries two untested assumptions, the flexure and the printer.
- The pivot trigger is written down: if the flexure coupon or the printer question fails by 2026-09-16, the team pivots to the insert, which needs no printer, and the meeting that day decides.
- The demo is the adaptive canopy against a fixed roof under one moving lamp, because a closed roof letting in less light is not a question, and adaptive against fixed is, see [the frontrunner tweaks](../research/frontrunner-tweaks.md).
- `project-brief.md` at the repo root is now the source of truth for scope, and the demo path in it is read only once hacking starts.
- The bus stop stays the demo and the pitch, and other applications go on one "same leaf, other roofs" card, because judges reward a named user and the Houston heat number, see [applications and judge appeal](../research/adaptive-bus-stop-canopy/applications-and-judge-appeal.md).
- Sponsor fit is framing, not build scope, because no 2026 sponsor challenge is published yet.
  The three sponsor variants stay proposed until one is.
- Draft roles, to confirm in the next meeting, from [the demo backwards plan](../research/adaptive-bus-stop-canopy/demo-backwards-plan.md):
  shannon owns the leaves and flexures, ameya owns the frame, linkage, and the fixed control canopy, lily owns electronics, sensing, and the test rig, abba owns firmware, the display, the evidence, and the submission.

## Status changes

- adaptive-bus-stop-canopy: shortlisted -> chosen
- impact-insert: stays shortlisted, backup
- termite-mound-testbed: stays shortlisted, backup
