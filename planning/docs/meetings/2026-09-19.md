# Meeting 2026-09-19

Not a sit down meeting: these are the working decisions abba made on the first day of the event, recorded here so the team can confirm or reverse them.
`project-brief.md` is read only once hacking starts, so nothing below has been written into it, and where this note and the brief disagree the team settles it out loud.

## Decisions

- The screen draws the stop from the front, because that is the view the judge has of the rig and the view where the gaps between the leaves open and close.
- Each leaf on screen is a rib and a translucent sheet, blades resting and petals bent, because the roof must never read as a solid panel.
- The mockup is iterated in Claude Design against a 28 row scorecard in `software/mockup-prompt.md`, capped at 7 rounds, because "premium" cannot be checked and a scorecard can.
- The pitch is told through impact on human life, the theme of the keynote and of one challenge, and Rosa stays the one hero, because three heroes is no hero.
- The farmworker on a rest break and the grower widen the vision beat and card 3, because California law already demands full shade above 80 F and her summer is nearly rainless.
- The scripts give the study's own cause for the hot shelter, trapped heat in closed walls, before the moving sun, because the earlier wording credited the study with a claim it did not make.
- The rain answer is: sensing is solved, water shedding is untested, and a clear sheet under the leaves is the fallback, because building a water test costs engineer hours and proves nothing at full size.
- The Voloridge entry needs its own analysis of raw data, with Houston METRO ridership by stop as the pick, because replaying a typical year is a playback and not an insight.
- The Voloridge analysis takes its hours from the city picker and the stretch features and never from the app's core loop, because the demo is what every judge scores.

- Some hardware could not be sourced, so there are now two plans, live and simulated, in `plans/`, with one shared core built first, because the app reads the same serial format from a board or from a simulator and no hour is spent on a plan that loses.
- The choice between them is made once, at 17:00 Saturday, venue hour 6, against five items in hand, because a pivot without an hour gets argued all night.
- The simulation is geometry from the team's CAD and real solar data, never leaf mechanics, and every figure from it is said as simulated, because the honesty rules matter more when nothing is measured.
- Devin, from Cognition, sponsors the event and the team is allowed to have it write the code, so abba plans the code and the agent builds from `plans/` and the local memory files, never from the labelled prototypes.

- The planning record is copied into the event repo, `flecto-stop`, under `planning/`, Markdown only, because the people and the build agent working there need the whole plan in one place, and the rule is about code, which is never copied.

- Plan B is chosen, ahead of the gate, because the team can demo the flapping leaf and nothing else: the stop, the sun, the sensing, and the reaction are simulated, and the simulation drives the real leaf through the contract's `S` command so it bends with the screen.
- The leaves in the simulation still react to brightness through a threshold and never track the sun's position, because that is the roof the team designed.
- The build agent's first task is R1, ninety minutes of research into rendering the stop in 3D and where the leaf's shape comes from, because a live buckling simulation is not viable and nobody should build one by accident.
- The demo day is proposed as 5 July 2013, the sunniest July day in the file, because the pitch opens in July and the sunniest day overall is 3 June, and abba confirms it before C6 is handed over.
- The work packages for the build agent live in `plans/packages/`, each with expected values taken from the real data, because the agent must not be able to pass by agreeing with its own mistake.

- At about 16:00 the team pivots from the bus stop canopy to a Flectofin leaf design tool, because the stop cannot be built, the leaf can, and ameya's design loop gives the team measured data and real hardware to stand on.
- The tool recommends a leaf's dimensions for a use case, from three beam formulas, a calibration against measured leaves, and a proposer for the next experiment, and it never fits a curve through a handful of leaves.
- The properties and the use cases are proposed in the idea file, three use cases and no more, and the team confirms or changes them at the 17:00 standup, which also closes the question of pivoting for the rest of the event.
- Plan B, the solar pipeline, the METRO analysis, and both sets of scripts are set aside, and the gates, the build agent's rules, and the link that drives the real leaf carry over.

- At about 16:45 the team pivots again, to a simulated smart greenhouse roof of Flectofin fins for a commercial grower, because the design tool had no user the team could picture and a roof that gives each crop its own light and rain does.
- It is simulated with one display fin, it waters by opening over dry zones when it rains, and it follows programmed targets per zone with no AI in the loop, because those were the smallest honest answers to four questions put to abba.
- This is the fourth plan of the day, so it is frozen at the 17:00 standup, and any later change is a cut and never a pivot.

- The setting is a shade house, which the team calls a greenhouse in plain speech, confirmed by abba, because closed greenhouses keep rain off the crop on purpose and a shade house does not.
- The sun comes from NASA POWER for the same place and year as the rain, because a typical year joined to a real year's rain made rainy hours brighter than dry ones.
- Houston is dropped and the place is Central Florida, the Apopka area, because the project no longer has anything to do with Houston, and a shade house region with summer afternoon storms puts sun and rain on the same days. The choice was made from memory and a planner is checking it against sources.
