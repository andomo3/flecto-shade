# Meeting 2026-09-19

Not a sit down meeting: these are the working decisions abba made on the first day of the event, recorded here so the team can confirm or reverse them.
`project-brief.md` is read only once hacking starts, so nothing below has been written into it, and where this note and the brief disagree the team settles it out loud.

## Decisions

- The screen draws the stop from the front, because that is the view the judge has of the rig and the view where the gaps between the leaves open and close.
- Each leaf on screen is a rib and a translucent sheet, blades resting and petals bent, because the roof must never read as a solid panel.
- The mockup is iterated in Claude Design against a 28 row scorecard in `software/mockup-prompt.md`, capped at 7 rounds, because "premium" cannot be checked and a scorecard can.
- The pitch is told through impact on human life, the theme of the keynote and of one challenge, and Rosa stays the one hero, because three heroes is no hero.
- The farmworker on a rest break and the grower widen the vision beat and card 3, because California law already demands full shade above 80 F and her summer is nearly rainless.
- The scripts give the study's own cause for the hot shelter, trapped heat in closed walls, before the moving sun, because the earlier wording credited the study with a claim it did not make.
- The rain answer is: sensing is solved, water shedding is untested, and a clear sheet under the leaves is the fallback, because building a water test costs engineer hours and proves nothing at full size.
- The Voloridge entry needs its own analysis of raw data, with Houston METRO ridership by stop as the pick, because replaying a typical year is a playback and not an insight.
- The Voloridge analysis takes its hours from the city picker and the stretch features and never from the app's core loop, because the demo is what every judge scores.

- Some hardware could not be sourced, so there are now two plans, live and simulated, in `plans/`, with one shared core built first, because the app reads the same serial format from a board or from a simulator and no hour is spent on a plan that loses.
- The choice between them is made once, at 17:00 Saturday, venue hour 6, against five items in hand, because a pivot without an hour gets argued all night.
- The simulation is geometry from the team's CAD and real solar data, never leaf mechanics, and every figure from it is said as simulated, because the honesty rules matter more when nothing is measured.
- Devin, from Cognition, sponsors the event and the team is allowed to have it write the code, so abba plans the code and the agent builds from `plans/` and the local memory files, never from the labelled prototypes.
