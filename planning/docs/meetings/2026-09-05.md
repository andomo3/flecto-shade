# Meeting 2026-09-05

The team read the v2 scoring, the termite research, and the frontrunner tweaks, and closed the field to three.

## Decisions

- The final three are the impact insert, the termite mound testbed, and the adaptive bus stop canopy, because they lead the v2 scoring at 40, 36, and 30 of 45 and each has a build path on the HackMIT hardware list.
- The termite mound testbed replaces the termite builder, because the builder is capped near 31 with no 2026 track while the mound keeps the termite story, fits Sustainability, and needs no robot.
- The v2 weighting is adopted for all further scoring: Demoable and Buildable count double, Prior art peaks at raw 3, Freshness at raw 4, maximum 45, because the team judged working and finishable to be decisive and fully novel ideas to be high risk in 24 hours.
- The evolved biomimetic structures, the gecko foot, and the morphing tensegrity are dropped, because none made the final three.
- The proposed but unresearched ideas, Bouligand drop rig, fog harvester, forest metabarrier, and kirigami morphing, are closed, which settles the carried over item from 2026-09-04.
- The final choice between the three waits on the tests below, because the mound and the canopy each carry one untested physical assumption and the insert carries none.

## Who researches what next

- Hardware teammate: mound kitchen test, two cardboard boxes, one bank of ten quarter watt resistors on 5 V, two temperature probes, thirty minutes, report whether the chimney box reads measurably cooler at the base, due next meeting.
- Hardware teammate: canopy heat test, a halogen or incandescent desk lamp over a probe in a black painted foam board figure, report the rise in five minutes shaded against unshaded, due next meeting.
- Embedded teammate: confirm which listed IMU streams accelerometer data at or near 1 kHz over USB, the MPU-6050 or MPU-9250 expected, and note the BNO055 rate for comparison, due next meeting.
- CAD teammate: print one TPU flexure coupon and one canopy leaf at home if any printer is reachable, and report whether the flexure survives ten bends, due next meeting.
