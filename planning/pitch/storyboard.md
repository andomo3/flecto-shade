# Demo storyboard

The golden path only: one grower, one day, ninety seconds from the opening word to the close.
Each beat says what is spoken, what the table does, and what the screen shows, so the pitch and the page are built from one table.
The beats, the times, and the quoted lines are the ninety second table in `../plans/plan-d-louvre-roof.md`, beat for beat, with one change from the team lead on the evening of 2026-09-19: there is no hardware, so the beat at 0:20 says what a fin is while the screen holds still, and nobody holds anything.
The screen column is checked against `../plans/packages/H2-page.md`, `H3-result-card.md`, and the demo gate in `../plans/gates.md`.

Pre-set before every judge: the page on its state before Play, the roof shut, the day rewound, the browser full screen.
Reset after every judge: one control, "Reset the day", the `R` key, under five seconds.
The day is 3 June 2023, named on the screen with its date.
Every figure spoken is provisional, from the planner's run in `../plans/packages/H1-zones-light-rain-soil.md`, and is confirmed against `data/processed/headline.json` before it is said.

## The clock, and where the sources disagree

`H2-page.md` plays a dark hour in half a second and a daylight hour in 2.4 seconds, and the demo gate allows 36 to 42 seconds from Play to the card.
On 3 June 2023 that puts the fern shutting 15 seconds after Play, the hydrangea 17.4, the storm starting 24.6, and the card at 38.6, computed from the real solar file for that day.
So the screen keeps to the times in beats 4 to 7, and the speaker still follows the screen, never the clock: each line is said when the screen shows it.
A flat 2.5 seconds an hour was set aside on Saturday evening, because it opened the demo with 15 seconds of night and put the spoken run nearer 1:50 than 1:30.
If the page as built runs to its full minute, the spoken run is nearer 1:50 than 1:30, and the team either accepts that or has the page start the day at sunrise, which is abba's decision and a change to H2, not to this file.

One more, for abba to settle in H2: the demo gate wants not one digit on the page before Play, and the footer's credit carries the patent number and the rain source carries the gauge's number.
Either the footer spells them only after Play, or the gate's words change, and beat 2 says the patent number aloud either way.

## Variations

- **H3 not merged:** beat 6 shows H2's plain table of the three zones' end of day values, beat 7 has no year view, and the fifty four percent is said only if `headline.json` exists to back it.
- **The roof layout file absent:** each zone draws the default grid, the footer says "default layout", and nothing spoken changes.
- **The second view, fin by fin, if it ships:** offered only after beat 7, to a judge who asks how the roof is laid out.
  It never enters the ninety seconds.
- **The recorded run:** the same beats and the same words, opened with one sentence, "this is a recorded run of the same day, from the same simulation", and the speaker presses Play if the judge cannot.
- **The screen recording:** the speaker narrates beats 3 to 7 over it, and says once that it is a recording.

## The judge touches it once

The playbook's booth rule is that the judge touches the project within thirty seconds.
Here it is the Play button at 0:30, and it is the only thing on the table to touch, because nothing physical was built.
It makes the judge the one who started the day, and the words under each zone make them a witness to the reasons.
