# Demo storyboard

The golden path only: one rider, one day, under ninety seconds from the first word to the number.
Each beat says what is spoken, what the table does, and what the screen shows, so the pitch, the hardware, and the UI are built from one page.
The screen column is the source for `../software/ui-brief.md`.

Pre-set before every judge: leaves resting, LED off, the app on its opening state, the day rewound to before sunrise.
Reset after every judge: one button, "Reset the day", under five seconds.

## Variations

- **Servo mounted sun:** in beats 4 to 7 the lamp also travels across the stop, and the screen's sun marker arcs instead of sliding.
  Nothing else changes.
- **Fixed LED mount:** the words "the sun moves" in beat 4 become "the sun brightens", and the screen already tells that story with the bar and the sky colour.
- **Backup fixed roof built:** beat 8 gains a third figure, "Z% under a fixed roof", and beat 5 gains a third bar that does not drop.
- **Replay mode:** a banner reads "Replay mode: the app is driving the leaves from the data", beat 6 is skipped, and the speaker says so.
- **The city picker, if it ships:** offered only after beat 9, to a judge who asks "does it work elsewhere?".
  It never enters the ninety seconds.

## The judge touches it twice

The playbook's booth rule is that the judge touches the project within thirty seconds.
Here it is the Play button at 0:25, and the hand over the sensor at 0:50.
The first makes them a participant, and the second makes them a witness.
