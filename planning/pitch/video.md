# Demo video plan

Two videos from one shoot.
Planning them now is allowed, and filming cannot start before the hardware exists, which is after 11:00 on Saturday.

| Video | Length | Job | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|---|
| The backup clip | 30 to 45 seconds, no voice needed | Played at the table the moment the live demo fails, so the pitch never stops | On the demo laptop's desktop and on a phone, because the venue Wi-Fi cannot be trusted, and on YouTube as unlisted |
| The submission video | 90 seconds to 2 minutes, confirm the limit on the 2026 form | What judges and recruiters watch when we are not there, and what the README embeds | YouTube, unlisted until judging ends, linked from the submission and the README |

The playbook's rules for the demo moment apply to both: a story and not a tour, the transformation and not the process, an emotional beat at the end, and under sixty seconds of actual demo.

## The clock

The venue closes from 01:00 to 07:00 on Sunday, and submission is at 11:00, so the video cannot wait for a perfect rig.

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Saturday, the first time light makes a leaf move, around hour 4 to 6 | Film ten seconds on a phone, whatever it looks like. From this moment a backup exists |
| Saturday, after the hour 12 checkpoint, 23:00 to 00:45 | **The safety take.** The full sequence, every shot on the list below, with the rig as it is. This is the take the submission video is cut from if Sunday goes badly |
| Sunday, 01:00 to 07:00, away from the venue | Cut the safety take into both videos, record the voiceover somewhere quiet, write the captions. A finished video exists before the venue reopens |
| Sunday, 09:00 to 09:30, after tuning | **The final take**, only the shots that improved: the wide shot, the leaves, the hand over the sensor. Drop them into the existing edit |
| Sunday, by 10:00 | Both videos exported, uploaded, the links in the submission and the README, local copies on the laptop and a phone |
| Sunday, 10:00 to 11:00 | Nothing touches the video. This hour belongs to the submission form |

If Sunday morning is on fire, the safety take ships, and that is why it exists.

## The words

The submission video uses the 60 second script from `script.md` as its voiceover, trimmed to the shots.
Record the voice separately, in a quiet room, on a phone held close, because a hackathon hall is too loud for any microphone.
Burn in captions, because judges often watch with the sound off.

Title card: the project name and the one sentence, over shot 1.
End card: the closing line, then the repo link and the team's names.
No team introduction, no "hi, we are", no architecture diagram.
If the study figures appear as text on screen, the source appears under them in small type.

## Honesty in the edit

- The leaves are never sped up, and the bend is never cut, because that shot is the claim.
- If replay mode was needed for the take, a caption says so.
- The numbers on the result card are the day's real numbers.
- No stock footage of buses, cities, or people in the heat unless its licence is checked and credited.
- Music is optional, and only from a source that permits it, credited in the description.

## Kit and settings

- A phone, filmed in landscape, 1080p at 30 frames a second, exposure and focus locked.
- Something to hold it still: a small tripod, or a stack of books and tape.
- One plain dark backdrop behind the model, a sheet of black foam board is enough, so the LED reads as the sun.
- Room lights down for shots 5 to 9 if the hall allows it, or a box around the model.
- Screen recording on the laptop: OBS, or the Windows game bar.
- Editing: Clipchamp ships with Windows 11, and CapCut or DaVinci Resolve also work.
  Install and open the editor once before Saturday so nothing downloads at the venue.
- Charge the phone, and clear storage before the safety take.

## Checks before it ships

- Watched once with the sound off: does it still make sense?
- Watched once by someone who has not seen the project: can they say what it does after one viewing?
- The backup clip plays from the laptop with the Wi-Fi off.
- The YouTube links open in a private window.
- The first frame works as a thumbnail, because the README shows it.
