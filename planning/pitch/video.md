# Demo video plan

Two videos from one screen recording.
There is no hardware and nothing to film on a table, so both are made from the page itself, and recording cannot start before the page plays the day, which is package H2.

| Video | Length | Job | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|---|
| The backup clip | About 75 seconds, no voice needed | Played at the table the moment both the page and the recorded run fail, so the pitch never stops. It is the "screen recording" of the fallback gate in `../plans/gates.md` | On the demo laptop's desktop and on a phone, because the venue Wi-Fi cannot be trusted, and on YouTube as unlisted |
| The submission video | 90 seconds to 2 minutes, confirm the limit on the 2026 form | What judges and recruiters watch when we are not there, and what the README embeds | YouTube, unlisted until judging ends, linked from the submission and the README |

The playbook's rules for the demo moment apply to both: a story and not a tour, the transformation and not the process, an emotional beat at the end, and about a minute of actual demo.

## The clock

The venue closes from 01:00 to 07:00 on Sunday, and submission is at 11:00, so the video cannot wait for a perfect page.
The times follow the clock in `../../CHECKLIST.md`.

| When | What |

| Saturday, before the freeze at 01:00 | **The safety take.** One full play of 3 June 2023, from the state before Play to the end of day table, with the page as it is. This is the take the submission video is cut from if Sunday goes badly |
| Sunday, 01:00 to 07:00, away from the venue | Cut the safety take into both videos, record the voiceover somewhere quiet, write the captions. A finished video exists before the venue reopens |
| Sunday, 07:00 to 09:00, after H3 is merged | **The final take**, with the result card and the year by month. Drop it into the existing edit, and record again any spoken figure that changed |
| Sunday, by 10:00 | Both videos exported, uploaded, the links in the submission and the README, local copies on the laptop and a phone |
| Sunday, 10:00 to 11:00 | Nothing touches the video. This hour belongs to the submission form |

If Sunday morning is on fire, the safety take ships, and that is why it exists.

## The words

The submission video uses the 90 second script in `video-script-90s.md`, which carries its own shot list for the page as built and replaces the shot list above where the two differ.
Record the voice separately, in a quiet room, on a phone held close, because a hackathon hall is too loud for any microphone.
Burn in captions, because judges often watch with the sound off.

Title card: the project name, the one sentence, and the word "simulated", over shot 1.
End card: the closing line, then the repo link and the team's names.
No team introduction, no "hi, we are", no architecture diagram.
Where the 50,000 square feet or the 8 and 12 mol appear as text on screen, the source appears under them in small type.

## Honesty in the edit

- The playback is never sped up, and shots 5 and 6 are never cut inside, because those shots are the claim.
- The word "simulated" is on the screen from the opening frame to the last.
- The figures on the result card are the build's own, from `headline.json`, and the voiceover says the same values.
- The fin is credited to ITKE in a caption and in the description.
- No footage of a real greenhouse, a real grower, or a real fin, because we have none, and no stock footage unless its licence is checked and credited.
- Nothing in the edit says or implies a word from the list the team never says, in `../plans/pitch-plan-d.md`.
- Music is optional, and only from a source that permits it, credited in the description.

## Kit and settings

- Screen recording on the laptop: OBS, or the Windows game bar, at 1440 by 900, 30 frames a second, the browser full screen, notifications off.
- A phone, for the voiceover only.
- Editing: Clipchamp ships with Windows 11, and CapCut or DaVinci Resolve also work.
  Open the editor once before the safety take so nothing downloads at the venue.

## Who

Abba records and edits, because the page is abba's package.
Whoever speaks at the table records the voiceover, so the video and the booth sound the same.

## Checks before it ships

- Watched once with the sound off: does it still make sense?
- Watched once by someone who has not seen the project: can they say what it does after one viewing?
- The backup clip plays from the laptop and from the phone with the Wi-Fi off.
- The YouTube links open in a private window.
- The opening frame works as a thumbnail, because the README shows it.
- Every figure heard or seen is in `headline.json`.
