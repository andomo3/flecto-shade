# Placards

Three cards and a QR, printed or hand lettered at the event, standing on the table where a judge can read them without asking.
They do the work of a slide deck at a booth, and they keep the pitch honest while the speaker is mid sentence.
Large type, few words, readable from a metre.
Square brackets are filled in at the event.

## Card 1: Measured today

> **Measured today, on this table**
>
> Light reaching the bench, as a share of the light arriving at the roof.
>
> Leaves bent: **[X] %**
> Leaves resting: **[Y] %**
> [Under a fixed roof: **[Z] %**, only if the backup roof was built]
>
> [N] readings each, [N] full cycles, [date], under an LED sun.

## Card 2: Not yet claimed

> **Not yet claimed**
>
> - Temperature or heat stress. We measured light.
> - Wind, rain, or durability.
> - A full size shelter, or its cost.
> - That riders want it. We have not asked them yet.
>
> The sun on this table is an LED playing one real Houston day from public solar data.

This card is the one judges remember, because almost nobody else has one.

## Card 3: Same leaf, other roofs

> **Same leaf, other roofs**
>
> A farmworker's rest shade. A grower's crop. A schoolyard queue.
> Anyone who is in the sun and cannot leave.
>
> One motor. No hinges. Nothing to seize.

Three uses, by the playbook's rule of three, and no hardware for any of them.

## Card 4: Why this matters

> **Houston, summer 2023**
>
> Researchers measured 17 bus stops.
> The worst shelter was 5.2 F hotter than standing in the open.
> About 1 in 6 Houston stops has a shelter at all.
>
> Sources: Lanza, Ernst, Watkins and Chen, 2025, Transportation Research Part D. City of Houston bus stop layer, 2022.

The exact figures live here, so the spoken pitch can round them.
Each figure is read once more at its source before the card is made.

## The QR

One code, to the event repo, whose README carries the video, the numbers, and the link to the public planning repo.
Test it with two different phones.

## Making them

Card stock or foam board offcuts from the rig, a thick marker, and a ruler.
If the venue has a printer, print them, and if not, hand lettering reads as craft and not as a shortcut.
Cards 2, 3, and 4 can be worded now, and card 1 waits for the measurement run.
